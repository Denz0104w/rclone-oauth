Privacy Policy

Application: Personal rclone Drive

Personal rclone Drive is used exclusively by its owner for personal Google Drive access and synchronization through rclone.

Data Access and Use

The application requests Google Drive API permissions solely to read, organize, and synchronize files in the owner's own Google Drive account from the owner's local device.

Data Collection and Sharing

No personal data is collected, stored on external servers, sold, or shared with third parties. OAuth tokens and configuration settings remain stored locally in the owner's rclone setup.

Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account third-party permissions settings.
